Case study

CareOne: rebuilding motor claims around the first ten minutes

After an accident, customers had to call a helpline, wait on hold, and repeat the same details three times. We moved the first notice of loss into a guided, photo-first flow that works on a cracked phone screen in bad light.

We tested early prototypes with surveyors and garage partners, not just policyholders, because the claim isn't over until the car is back on the road. Read the full process write-up for the research plan and the three ideas we killed.

Paper · Abstract

Designing for low-literacy insurance customers in tier-3 cities

We report a mixed-methods study (n = 42) of how first-time policyholders interpret claim-status messages. Participants consistently over-trusted progress bars and under-read text, which suggests that status UI may create false certainty. We propose three patterns — plain-verb states, expected-next-step lines, and human handoff cues — and discuss their limits.
